    Authorization to Access Credit Monitoring Account

    I authorize Kristal J Consulting LLC (“KJC”) to log in to my MyScoreIQ account using the username and password I provided, for the purposes below.

    What KJC may do

    • View and download my credit reports and FICO scores from Experian, Equifax and TransUnion.
    • Review them each month while I am a KJC client to prepare findings, recommendations and dispute documents for my approval.

    What KJC will not do

    • Change my password, email, phone number, security settings, payment method or plan.
    • Apply for credit, open or close accounts, or contact creditors or credit bureaus in my name without my separate written approval.
    • Share my reports or login with anyone outside KJC without my written approval.

    My account

    I own the MyScoreIQ account and pay its fees directly. KJC is not responsible for MyScoreIQ charges.

    Security

    KJC stores my login encrypted and uses it only for the purposes above. Copies of reports pulled during my engagement are kept in my client file and deleted on my written request, unless the law requires KJC to keep them.

    Ending access

    I can revoke this authorization at any time by emailing credit@kristaljconsulting.com and changing my MyScoreIQ password. KJC will stop accessing the account and delete my stored login within five business days. Access also ends automatically when my engagement with KJC ends.
